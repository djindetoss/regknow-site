Abstract

This publication provides a high-level overview of key regulatory considerations for the safe and appropriate development, evaluation, deployment and oversight of artificial intelligence (AI) systems for health. Developed through the WHO–International Telecommunication Union Focus Group on AI for Health Working Group on Regulatory Considerations, it is intended as a resource for developers, regulators, policy-makers, manufacturers and health practitioners rather than as a regulatory framework or formal guidance. The document examines six principal topic areas: documentation and transparency; risk management across the total product lifecycle; intended use and analytical and clinical validation; data quality; privacy and data protection; and engagement and collaboration. The publication emphasizes that trustworthy AI for health requires transparent documentation, proportionate risk management, rigorous validation, high-quality data, protection of privacy and continuous collaboration among stakeholders throughout the AI system lifecycle. It highlights the importance of post-market monitoring, quality management, cybersecurity, and adaptation to diverse health-care settings while recognizing the rapid evolution of AI technologies. The report concludes by encouraging continued international dialogue, regulatory convergence and the ongoing refinement of frameworks and best practices to ensure that AI systems are safe, effective and capable of improving health outcomes across different contexts.
