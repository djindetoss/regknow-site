Abstract

Abstract Early detection and prompt treatment of drug-resistant tuberculosis continue to represent the “holy grail” in tuberculosis research. Advances in molecular methods have drastically reduced turnaround times but novel strategies for early identification of drug-resistant tuberculosis (DR-TB) are still needed. We combined the diversity, representativeness and volume of the TB Portals chest X-ray image set with the power of deep learning and machine learning to develop and train an ensemble model for prediction of DR-TB. Using 7,151 chest X-ray images, confirmed drug-resistance diagnosis, our ensemble model achieved an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) of 0.8253 and 0.8108 in the validation and held-out test subsets. This classification performance correlated with the extent of drug resistance such that extensive drug resistance patients showed a higher predicted probability than patients with multigrug resistance. The model’s predicted probabiliy also correlated with adverse treatment outcome and was robust to potential demographic confounders. Presence of comorbidities diminished the AUC of the ensemble model. SHapley Additive exPlanations-value based explainer demonstrated that the model focused accurately on the lung fields. If replicated through diverse set of external data, our ensemble model will provide a gatekeeper screening protocol for early risk stratification of drug-resistant tuberculosis.
